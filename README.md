Senior full-stack engineer and technical lead. Most of my last five years has gone into the unglamorous parts of scale — making synchronization durable, turning brittle event chains into orchestration you can reason about, and cutting the duplication that slows a team down. I've been promoted to lead in one to two months at three different places, and I think that's because I never leave the code.

### `what I've been building`

**🏗️ Recruitment platform at scale** — Led 5 engineers on a multi-tenant, AI-powered platform serving 40k+ candidates across multiple firms. The interesting problems lived in the plumbing:

- Migrated fragile sync workflows to **Temporal** — durable orchestration across hundreds of long-running processes, replacing brittle event chains
- Re-architected the event pipeline from SQS-only to **AWS Kinesis** — multi-consumer broadcasting across thousands of events/day
- Built secure video-interview infra on **AWS IVS + Lambda@Edge** with edge-level access control and proctoring
- Stood up a **NestJS + Next.js monorepo** with shared packages and micro-frontends — cut code duplication **~70%**
- Rebuilt CI/CD from scratch with **AWS CDK** as the single source of truth for infrastructure

**⚡ Before that** — Elasticsearch-powered e-commerce search with attribute-based PIM · an institutional information system in ASP.NET Core + Angular secured with OpenID Connect · real-time logistics tracking in Flutter over persistent WebSockets · and a digital agency I co-founded and scaled to five engineers.
